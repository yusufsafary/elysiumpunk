Not financial advice

Nothing ELYSIUM PUNKS publishes counts as advice. Not on this site, not in the docs, not in the skill files, not on social media. Nothing here is financial, investment, legal or tax advice, and nothing is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold anything.

Collectibles with volatile prices

Punks are collectibles. People collect them because they like them. If a secondary market price exists, buyers and sellers set it, not us. It can go to zero. Nothing backs it, pegs it or guarantees it, and there may simply never be a buyer for your punk at any price.

No promised utility or roadmap value

What you get is the artwork, the type your seed rolled, and an NFT that stays on-chain. Anything beyond that (community things, events, extras) is at our discretion, can change or never happen, and isn't part of what you're buying. Don't mint because you're expecting future benefits.

Smart contract and chain risk

The collection runs on smart contracts on the Elysium chain, a new network in the Hyperliquid ecosystem. New chain, new contracts, real risk. Bugs happen. Networks go down. Bridges and sequencers fail, ecosystems change their minds. Any of that can cut you off from your NFTs or hurt their value, and losses from it can't be reversed.

Third parties

Your wallet, OpenSea, X, GitHub Pages and Google Fonts are all run by somebody else. We don't control them, and we're not responsible for their outages, their policies or what they do with your data. And the obvious one, because it keeps happening: scam accounts impersonate NFT projects for a living. Trust only links published on elysiumpunk.fun, and never, ever hand your seed phrase to anyone.

Your call

So: do your own research. Use a wallet you control. Mint only with money you can afford to lose completely. And if anything on this page sits badly with you, the right move is simple. Don't mint.
