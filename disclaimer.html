A plain, honest summary of the risks, in normal language. Minting is optional and entirely your call.

Not financial advice

Nothing published by ELYSIUM PUNKS, on this site, in the docs, in the skill files, or on social accounts, is financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. No content is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold anything.

Collectibles with volatile prices

Punks are digital collectibles made for collecting. Their secondary market price, if any, is set entirely by buyers and sellers, can move to zero, and is not backed, pegged, or guaranteed by the project. There may be no buyer for your punk at any price.

No promised utility or roadmap value

The collection stands on its artwork, its types, and its on-chain permanence. Any community features, events, or extras are discretionary, may change, and are not part of a purchase contract. Do not mint expecting future benefits.

Smart contract and chain risk

The collection runs on smart contracts on the Elysium chain, a new network built on the Hyperliquid ecosystem. New chains and new contracts carry real risk: bugs, downtime, bridge or sequencer failures, and ecosystem changes can affect access to or the value of your NFTs, and losses from such events cannot be reversed.

Third parties

Wallets, OpenSea, X, GitHub Pages, and Google Fonts are third-party services. The project does not control them and is not responsible for their outages, policies, or handling of your data. Scam accounts impersonate NFT projects constantly: only trust links published on elysiumpunk.fun, and never share your seed phrase with anyone.

Your call

Do your own research, use a wallet you control, and mint only with funds you can afford to lose entirely. If any of this is unclear or unacceptable, the correct action is not to mint.
