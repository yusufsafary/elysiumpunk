Privacy policy

Your business
stays yours.

Last updated 9 November 2026. This is a static website. There are no accounts, nothing to sign up for, and nothing watching you browse. Here's what that means for your data, concretely.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no logins, and we never ask for your email.
  • No analytics, no ad trackers. Not now, not planned.
  • We don't sell, rent or share visitor data. There would be almost nothing to sell anyway.
  • No fingerprinting and no cross-site tracking.

One thing in your browser

The banner saves one local storage item, ep_cookie_consent, with your Accept or Decline answer and the date you gave it. It never leaves your browser. Its only job is to stop the banner from asking you the same question on every visit. The Cookie Policy has the details, and a button to change your answer.

Fonts and hosting

Fonts come from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Asking for a font file tells Google your IP address and browser details, the same as any file request on the web. We set no Google cookies. Hosting is GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep ordinary server logs of page requests, IP addresses included, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We don't get those logs, and we wouldn't use them for tracking if we did.

Wallets and minting

When mint opens, everything happens inside your own wallet. You connect, you read the transaction, you sign it. This website never asks for your seed phrase or private keys, and there's no way for it to receive them. If anyone asks for them while claiming to be this project, it's a scam. One thing that is public, by design: your wallet address and your on-chain activity. That's true for every collection on every chain, not just this one.

Leaving this site

Links to X and OpenSea lead to other companies' services. The second you follow one, their privacy and cookie policies are in charge, not this page. The whitelist tasks on X (follow, repost, comment) happen entirely on X, under your account there.

Changes and contact

If a future feature changes anything here, this page gets updated before or when the feature ships, and anything material gets announced on X. Privacy questions go to the project account on X. The handle isn't public yet; it'll be linked in the header and footer the moment it exists.
