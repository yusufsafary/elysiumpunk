Types and odds

At mint, an on-chain seed generates your punk. Type comes first, traits after. The percentages below are the odds the generator was built with, not a promise about any single mint.

TypeShareApprox. countMarks
MortalAbout 70%About 7,000Human skin, street traits only
BlessedAbout 27%About 2,700Halo or laurel
SaintAbout 3%About 300Golden skin and a halo

Trait system

Every face is half street, half afterlife. The street side: mohawk, spikes, long hair, flat top, cap, shades, gold frame glasses, gold tooth, grin, cigarette, beard, gold earring, gold chain. The paradise side: halo, laurel, golden skin. Backgrounds split the difference: gold fields, paradise sky, street wall, neon alley, marble. Put together across 9,999 seeds, no two faces come out alike.

Mint rules

  • Minting happens here, on this website. You connect a normal EVM wallet set to the Elysium chain and mint from it.
  • Whitelist wallets mint 1 punk free. Each free mint carries a flat $0.19 commission in the Elysium native token, charged to keep bots from farming the list. There are exactly 1,000 whitelist spots.
  • Registration opens with the announcement on X. You submit an EVM address, complete three tasks (follow, repost, comment), then confirm on this site and get a confirmation code.
  • Public mint costs $3.50 per punk in the chain's native token. Gas is paid in HYPE.
  • The mint date and the public per-wallet limit haven't been announced. Both will show up on X and on this page.

Reveal

Nobody sees their face at mint, not even us. Exactly 24 hours after mint, every punk reveals in the same moment: face, type and traits, all public at once.

Trading

After reveal, secondary trading lives on OpenSea. Minting never happens anywhere but here. So if someone offers to sell you a mint or a whitelist spot somewhere else before the official announcement, walk away. It isn't us.
