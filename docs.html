Types and odds

Every punk is generated from an on-chain seed at mint. The seed locks the type first, then the traits. Odds below are the design odds of the generator.

TypeShareApprox. countMarks
MortalAbout 70%About 7,000Human skin, street traits only
BlessedAbout 27%About 2,700Halo or laurel
SaintAbout 3%About 300Golden skin and a halo

Trait system

Each face mixes two worlds. Street traits come from the life the punk lived: mohawk, spikes, long hair, flat top, cap, shades, gold frame glasses, gold tooth, grin, cigarette, beard, gold earring, gold chain. Paradise traits come from the fields: halo, laurel, golden skin, and the backgrounds of gold fields, paradise sky, street wall, neon alley and marble. No two of the 9,999 faces are alike.

Mint rules

  • Mint happens on this website with a normal EVM wallet connected to the Elysium chain.
  • Whitelist wallets mint 1 punk free. There are exactly 1,000 whitelist spots.
  • Whitelist registration opens with the announcement on X: submit an EVM address, complete three tasks (follow, repost, comment), then confirm on this site and receive a confirmation code.
  • Public mint is $3.50 per punk, paid in the native token of the Elysium chain. Gas is paid in HYPE.
  • The mint date and the public per wallet limit are announced on X and on this page.

Reveal

Faces stay hidden after mint. Exactly 24 hours after mint, every punk reveals at once: face, type and traits become public together, for the whole collection at the same moment.

Trading

Secondary trading happens on OpenSea once the collection is live there after reveal. This website is the only place to mint. Anyone selling mints or whitelist spots anywhere else before the official announcement is not us.
