08 · Questions

Questions from
the afterlife.

When does mint open? +

Once the Elysium mainnet is up and the contracts are deployed. We haven't published a date yet, on purpose. When there is one, it goes on X and on this page first, and the mint button here wakes up. Until then it just sits there saying "opens soon", because it is.

What does a punk cost? +

Whitelist? Free. Well, nearly: there's a flat $0.19 commission in the Elysium native token on the free mint, and yes, it's there to annoy bots. Public mint is $3.50 a punk in the same token. Either way you also pay gas, and gas on Elysium is HYPE.

How does the whitelist work? +

It's open. You send us your EVM address on the whitelist page, you follow, repost and comment on X, then you confirm back on the site form and get a confirmation code. 1,000 spots total, and the first 1,000 verified entries are guaranteed.

When is the reveal? +

24 hours after you mint, to the minute. Your punk hides until then. Everybody's does. Then all the faces and types show up at once.

Where can I trade after mint? +

OpenSea. Once the reveal lands, the collection goes up there for secondary trading. Minting only ever happens here, though.

Which chain is this on? +

Elysium. It's the Kinetiq L2 built on Hyperliquid. You mint from a normal EVM wallet connected to this site, and gas is paid in HYPE.

How many can I mint in public sale? +

We haven't set the public limit yet. It gets announced with the mint date. The whitelist side is fixed, though: 1 free mint per wallet, no exceptions.

What is $EPUNK, and how do I get it? +

It is the collection's token, and you get it by burning a punk: 100,000 for a Mortal, 300,000 for a Blessed, 3,000,000 for a Saint, for a flat $0.19 fee in HYPE. Burning opens when public mint begins and stays open from then on, with no deadline. A burned punk is gone for good, so think before you feed one to the contract.
