04 / How Mint Works

Three steps
to paradise.

Minting happens here, on this website, with a normal EVM wallet. No other site, no other link. If a mint page for this collection lives anywhere else, it is not us.

1

Connect your wallet

Connect a normal EVM wallet on this site and switch it to the Elysium chain. Gas on Elysium is paid in HYPE.

2

Mint your punk

Whitelist wallets mint 1 punk free, plus a flat $0.19 commission in the Elysium native token, paid by the minter as an anti-bot measure. Public mint is $3.50 per punk, paid in the native token of the Elysium chain.

3

Reveal and trade

Your punk reveals exactly 24 hours after mint. After the reveal, secondary trading happens on OpenSea.

06 / FAQ

Questions from
the afterlife.

When does mint open? +

Mint opens after the Elysium mainnet launch. The exact date and time will be announced on X and on this page. The mint button stays in its "opens soon" state until contracts are live.

What does a punk cost? +

The punk itself is free for whitelist wallets. A flat $0.19 commission in the Elysium native token applies to the free mint, paid by the minter: cheap for a human, expensive at bot scale. Public mint is $3.50 per punk, paid in the native token of the Elysium chain. Gas on Elysium is paid in HYPE.

How does the whitelist work? +

Registration has not opened yet. When it opens, you will submit your EVM address, complete three tasks on X (follow, repost, comment), and confirm here on the site. There are 1,000 spots total, and the opening is announced on X first.

When is the reveal? +

Exactly 24 hours after mint. Every punk stays hidden until the reveal moment, then all faces and types show at once.

Where can I trade after mint? +

On OpenSea. The collection will be listed there for secondary trading once the reveal lands.

Which chain is this on? +

The Elysium chain, the Kinetiq L2 on Hyperliquid. You mint with a normal EVM wallet connected to this site, and gas is paid in HYPE.

How many can I mint in public sale? +

To be announced with the mint date. Whitelist is fixed at 1 free mint per wallet.
