Cookie policy

Small site.
Small print.

Last updated 9 November 2026. This is a short page, because there's almost nothing to put on it. Here's what elysiumpunk.fun stores in your browser, what it doesn't, and how to change your mind.

What this site stores

ItemKindPurposeLifetime
ep_cookie_consentLocal storage, first partyRemembers whether you accepted or declined on the cookie banner, so we do not ask on every visitUntil you clear it or change your choice

That's the whole list. One item. This site sets no advertising cookies, runs no analytics, and doesn't build a profile of you. And because minting happens inside your own wallet, your private keys and seed phrase never come anywhere near this site.

Third parties

Two honest exceptions, both outside our walls. Fonts load from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), and Google may log your IP address and browser details when those files are requested. We set no Google cookies ourselves. Links to X and OpenSea take you off this site entirely, and from that click on, their cookie and privacy policies are the ones that apply.

Your choice

Say yes or say no on the banner. The site works either way. Declining doesn't break anything; the banner just remembers your answer so it stops asking. Changed your mind later? Use the button below, or clear site data for elysiumpunk.fun in your browser settings.

Changes and contact

If a future feature ever needs to store something new, this page gets updated before or when it ships, and the banner will ask you again. Questions about any of this go to the project account on X, linked in the footer.
