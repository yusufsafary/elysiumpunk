Third parties

Fonts load from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Google may log your IP address and browser details when the font files are requested. Your choice

Accept or decline on the banner, either way the site works. Declining changes nothing today beyond the banner remembering your answer. You can change your choice at any time with the button below, or by clearing site data for elysiumpunk.fun in your browser settings.

Changes and contact

If minting or new features ever add storage to this list, this page is updated before or when the feature ships, and the banner will ask again. Questions about this policy go through the project account on X, linked in the footer.
