Minting and fees

  • Whitelist wallets mint 1 punk free. A flat $0.19 commission in the Elysium native token applies to each free mint, paid by the minter, as an anti-bot measure.
  • Public mint is $3.50 per punk in the Elysium native token, if and while public mint is open.
  • Network gas on Elysium is paid in HYPE and goes to the network, not to the project.
  • Blockchain transactions are final. A confirmed mint cannot be reversed, refunded, or edited by anyone, including the project.

Reveal

Every punk reveals exactly 24 hours after mint. Its type and traits are set by the on-chain seed at the moment of minting. After that, neither the project nor the holder can change them.

You can also use your own punk's artwork commercially, within a fair, good-faith scale.
